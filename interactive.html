The finding

Question: how far off is Newton's law of cooling over 30 minutes when its constant is fitted to one cup and carried to other cups?

Answer: on the seven held-out curves the mean 30-minute error is for Newton's law. The target set before the test was under 0.5 K, so the frozen test failed. The chart below draws the real per-curve values. Change the set or the ambient assumption to see how the numbers move.

          Protocol

            What the paper says it does not show

            • One measured coffee curve is the fitting curve, and only one held-out curve is coffee. Six of the eight curves are hot-water cups from one lab and protocol.
            • No source reports relative humidity or a lid, so evaporation is not tested.
            • Ambient temperature is assumed at 20 °C for the fitting curve and the Langer cups.
            • The exponent 2.18 is a single fitted estimate. It is not a physical constant, and it does not carry across cups without refitting.
            • Four of the ten Langer fits again reach the lower bound of the ambient search, which lowers confidence in those fitted-again errors.
