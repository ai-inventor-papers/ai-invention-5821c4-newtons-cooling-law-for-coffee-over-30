**Choices I made that you may want to change**
- **C1 is reparametrised as `kc` and `x`.** In `dT/dt = -a (T - T_room)^x / C`, only `a/C` is identifiable from a cooling curve, so the plan fits that combined rate.
- **The bootstrap differs by source.** The Langer curves get a bootstrap over curves. Sedlacek and Manitoba have one curve each, so their intervals are a block bootstrap over time points within the curve and are labelled as not comparable.
